System-to-user sentences: confirmations, errors, rate limits.

```jsx
<StatusMessage tone="success">Password changed. Your other devices have been signed out.</StatusMessage>
<StatusMessage tone="danger" plain>Invalid e-mail or password.</StatusMessage>
```

- Inside a form use `plain`; for a whole-card outcome use the tinted box.
- Copy comes from the product's dictionaries — full sentences, no exclamation marks.
